ICT 280 Project

Hello, my name is Jennifer and I like to travel and take pictures. I have traveled across the United States and have taken many photos. I only have 7 states that I have yet to travel to. I hope to be able to make them all someday. I have also been able to travel to countries other than the United States. When I was young, we traveled into Mexico, so I do not have pictures from that time. But in 2012, I was lucky enough to travel to England. I will be able to share some of my photos from there. I hope everyone will enjoy my photos from near and far.
